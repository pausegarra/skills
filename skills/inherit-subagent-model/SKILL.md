# Inherit Subagent Model

Whenever spawning or dispatching a subagent, use the parent's exact model and reasoning or thinking configuration.

- Do not downgrade, upgrade, or substitute the model.
- Do not change the reasoning or thinking level.
- Apply this rule recursively: every subagent must inherit the exact configuration of its immediate parent.
